WHY LEADGREEN EXISTS

Empowering Young African Change Makers

The LeadGreen Fellowship catalyzes the potential of young environmental changemakers across Africa. We empower emerging climate leaders by equipping them with practical skills, dedicated mentorship, and a powerful network of peers.

Designed for high-impact youth aged 20-30, our intensive program goes beyond traditional training. We accelerate the careers and initiatives of young Africans who are already driving bold, community-centered solutions for a sustainable continent.

A network of changemakers from 54 African countries taking action in their communities

Our impact

Through the program the fellows learn the environmental challenges affecting their community and continent through practical training, expert conversations and peer learning, take real environmental action through structured LeadGreen Missions designed to be achievable in their own community

WHAT YOU'LL EXPERIENCE

A journey from environmental awareness to action.

The program is online and self-paced and weekly program, designed to fit around your studies and your life. But it's not something you do alone. You'll move through 12 weeks as part of a global cohort, with live masterclasses, peer learning, and a community that extends well beyond graduation.

Environmental Foundations

Understand the environmental challenges facing Africa—from plastic pollution and unmanaged waste to deforestation, biodiversity loss, flooding, and climate change. Build the knowledge you need to understand problems in your own community and identify where you can make a difference.

Green Skills & Solutions

Learn practical approaches to solving environmental problems. Explore waste management, recycling, climate action, afforestation, sustainable living, and green technology while developing the skills to turn environmental challenges into opportunities.

Environmental Action Missions

This is where learning becomes action. Complete practical environmental missions in your community, document what you do, and share evidence of your impact. Every fellow is expected to move from “I care about the environment” to “I did something about it.”

Community & Impact

Connect with young Africans who are taking action for the planet. Collaborate, share ideas, learn from environmental practitioners, and build relationships that extend beyond the fellowship. Your journey is not just about completing a program—it's about becoming part of a growing movement of African environmental changemakers.

WHO IS THIS FOR

This program was built for you.

If you’re a university student or recent graduate, 18 to 29 years old, from a limited-income or first-generation background — you’re exactly who Leadgreen was designed for. The application takes 10 minutes. If you meet the criteria, you’re in.

To qualify, you must be 18-29 years old, enrolled in or recently graduated from university, and from a low-income or first-generation background. No prior leadership experience is strictly required, just a drive to make an impact.

Yes. The core 12-week fellowship is 100% free of charge to ensure there are no financial barriers for high-impact young change-makers from marginalized communities.

You can expect to spend approximately 3 hours per week engaging with the course materials, attending live peer sessions, and working on your community project framework.
