Registration Inquiries

Answers to common questions regarding fellowship accreditation and credential generation.

What does the $1.99 USD registration fee support?
The fee covers real-time cryptographic verification, secure high-resolution cloud PDF rendering, and lifetime registry record hosting across our international fellow directory.
Can I adjust the name formatting after completing payment?
Yes. In the Certificate Studio, you can format your name in Title Case, lowercase, or adjust accents directly on the live generator before generating your final file.
Are both English and French editions available?
Yes. You can select your preferred edition during registration. Both English and French credential templates are authorized and permanently verifiable.
How rapidly do I receive access to my credential?
Access is immediate. Upon payment confirmation through Coachli, you are redirected directly to the Certificate Studio where your document is generated for instant download.
