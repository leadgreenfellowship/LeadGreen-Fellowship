01 | Personal information Cohort V

Please provide details that will help us contact you and understand the geographic reach of the fellowship.

Before you begin: Please complete all required sections. Be specific and honest: we are interested in your actions, ideas, learning goals and willingness to contribute—not only your passion for the environment. Estimated completion time: 20–30 minutes.

Applicant Checklist *

02 | Education, work and skills Background

Your background does not have to be climate-related. We welcome different disciplines and experiences.

03 | Environmental engagement & solutions Action

You do not need to have worked for an organisation. Personal, community-led and informal initiatives count.

04 | Leadership and collaboration Teamwork

Leadership can mean taking initiative, helping a team move forward or making it easier for others to contribute.

05 | Motivation and learning goals Vision

Tell us why this fellowship is relevant to your journey and what you hope to contribute.

06 | Availability and mission commitment Commitment

Cohort V is designed around participation, collaboration and practical action.

07 | Collaborative Projects & Skills Profile Impact Labs

LeadGreen Impact Labs is the proposed collaborative project component of the fellowship. Fellows bring different skills together to research problems, build tools, develop ideas, create campaigns or implement community solutions. Your answers help us form balanced multidisciplinary teams. You do not need to be an expert to participate.
