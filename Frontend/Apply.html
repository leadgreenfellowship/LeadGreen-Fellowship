What You Can Prepare in Advance

When applications officially open, prospective fellows will be asked to complete an 8-stage profile. You can start assembling these materials now:

Headshot Portrait Photo

A recent, clear front-facing portrait photograph for your fellowship dossier and directory.

Education & Skill Strengths

Your academic background, core competencies, and specific technical skills you bring to a team.

Environmental Impact Track Record

Past community initiatives, tree planting, waste management, or renewable advocacy you contributed to.

Mission & Team Commitment

Availability to participate in 6–8 hours/week of live peer learning and execute a cross-border mission.
