OUR ORIGIN & VALUES

Building a Pan-African Community of Limitless Climate Leaders.

The LeadGreen Fellowship was founded on the fundamental belief that raw talent is evenly distributed across Africa, but opportunity is not. We exist to bridge that gap.

FOUNDED IN 2023

Leadership Is An Action, Not A Title.

Africa is home to the youngest demographic in the world. Yet across our cities and rural communities, youth face the steepest economic challenges and the most severe environmental crises.

We built LeadGreen to turn eco-anxiety into community agency: equipping students and young professionals with the practical skills and networks needed to lead real environmental change.

LeadGreen began with a clear realization: African youth do not need another theoretical seminar. They need structured platforms where learning transforms immediately into field evidence.

Since our first cohort, we have prioritized localized, grassroots execution. Our fellows do not merely write essays on renewable energy or municipal plastic waste—they organize collection networks, build biodigesters, run afforestation drives, and engage municipal councils.

Today, LeadGreen unites changemakers from 54 African nations into a cohesive, supportive peer community that operates with zero financial barrier for deserving candidates.

OUR VISION

A Thriving, Ecologically Sustainable Africa.

We envision an Africa where every young person, in every community, possesses the agency, tools, and opportunity to shape an ecologically sustainable and thriving future—for themselves and for generations to come.

OUR MISSION

Accelerating African-Led Climate Solutions.

To accelerate homegrown African climate action by strategically investing in the leadership of youth and the innovation of grassroots communities through rigorous training, verified field missions, and ongoing peer mentorship.

OUR CORE VALUES

Integrity, Inclusivity, and Bias Towards Action.

We operate with zero tolerance for discrimination, hate, or prejudice. We believe in open knowledge, radical empathy, scientific integrity, and unyielding support for changemakers from low-income and first-generation backgrounds.

CONTINENTAL SCOPE

Uniting Emerging Leaders Across 54 African Countries.

From Dakar to Nairobi, Cairo to Cape Town, LeadGreen connects young environmental champions across linguistic, cultural, and national boundaries into a unified front for planetary stewardship.
