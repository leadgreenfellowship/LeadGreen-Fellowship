LeadGreen Fellowship

The People Behind the Mission

Built by Africans, for Africa — a small, determined team channelling extraordinary grassroots ambition into lasting continental change.

Est.
2024

Small team. Continental ambition.

LeadGreen Fellowship was built on a simple conviction: Africa's most pressing environmental challenges will be solved by Africans — specifically, the young people who live closest to the land, the rivers, and the communities at greatest risk from climate volatility.

Our team is a cross-disciplinary group of educators, environmental practitioners, programme designers, and community organisers, united by one goal: to build the most credible and impactful youth climate fellowship on the continent.

"Leadership is not a title — it is on-the-ground action, measurable impact, and community stewardship."

The numbers

Five people. One continent's worth of change.

From a founding team of five, LeadGreen Fellowship has built the infrastructure to credential, mentor, and deploy climate leaders across every region of Africa. Every fellow is proof of what a small, committed team can build when it refuses to compromise on ambition.

How we work

The values that
guide every decision

Radical Transparency

Every decision, every credential, every impact claim is documented and open to scrutiny. We believe accountability is the foundation of trust.

Grassroots First

We build from the community up. The best solutions come from people who understand their environments, not from outside institutions imposing theory.

Measurable Impact

We hold ourselves to the same standard we hold our fellows: every programme must produce evidence. Good intentions are not enough.

African Ownership

Africa's environmental future belongs to Africans. We are not a chapter of a foreign organisation — we are built on the continent, for the continent.
