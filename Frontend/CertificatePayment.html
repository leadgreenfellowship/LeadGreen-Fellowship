Why is there a $1.99 verification fee?

The nominal $1.99 USD fee directly covers tamper-evident digital registry serialization, lifetime cloud hosting, and cryptographic QR code verification maintenance so universities and employers can verify your credential worldwide.

When will I receive my certificate?

Immediately upon completing payment! Your email is verified in our database and you will receive instant access to the Certificate Studio to preview, sign, and download your high-resolution vector PDF and print-ready files.

Yes! Your certificate is issued with an official Credential ID linked directly to the public LeadGreen registry. You can add it directly to your LinkedIn profile under "Licenses & Certifications" with a single click.
