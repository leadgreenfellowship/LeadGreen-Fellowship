OUR STORY

Building a community of limitless leaders.

The Leadgreen fellowship was founded on the belief that raw talent is evenly distributed, but opportunity is not. We exist to bridge that gap.

LeadGreen was founded in 2023 with a simple realization: Africa has the youngest population in the world and is the continent most vulnerable to social challenges and climate change. Yet, young people often lack the structured support to lead the transition.

We bridge that gap. We believe that leadership isn't a title—it's an action. Our organization exists to unlock the potential of African youth to become stewards of their environment.

VISION

We envision an Africa where every young person, in every community, possesses the agency, tools, and opportunity to shape a sustainable and thriving future—for themselves and for generations to come.

MISSION

To accelerate African-led climate solutions by strategically investing in the leadership of youth and the innovation of grassroots communities.

VALUE STATEMENT

We exist to accelerate homegrown African climate solutions by investing in the agency of youth leaders and the ingenuity of grassroots innovators. We are building a global, inclusive, and empathetic community that operates with zero tolerance for discrimination, hate, or prejudice.

LEADGREEN

Learn. Act. Measure. Lead.

Building a network of young African changemakers taking measurable action for a greener Africa.
