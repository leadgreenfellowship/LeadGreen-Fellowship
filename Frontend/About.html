Our Story & Mission

Building Africa's Foremost Network of Climate Leaders

The LeadGreen Fellowship exists to accelerate homegrown African climate solutions by investing in the agency of youth and the ingenuity of grassroots innovators across the continent.

Founded on Purpose
"Leadership is not a title—it is on-the-ground action, measurable impact, and community stewardship."

LeadGreen was founded with a foundational insight: Africa has the youngest population on earth and faces the most severe vulnerabilities from climate volatility. Yet, youth innovators frequently lack structured mentorship, verifiable serialization, and funding opportunities to scale their grassroots solutions. We bridge that divide by connecting ambitious changemakers with rigorous curriculum, project mentoring, and permanent credentialing.

Our Vision

A Thriving, Climate-Resilient African Future

We envision an Africa where every young person, in every community, possesses the agency, tools, and opportunity to shape an ecologically sustainable and economically vibrant future for generations to come.

Our Mission

Accelerating African-Led Environmental Action

To accelerate grassroots climate innovations by strategically investing in youth mentorship, practical project execution, verified credentialing, and international fellowship opportunities.

Value Statement

Integrity, Diversity & Tangible Impact

We operate with absolute transparency, mutual respect, and zero tolerance for discrimination. We champion locally grounded climate solutions led by communities who understand their ecosystems best.
